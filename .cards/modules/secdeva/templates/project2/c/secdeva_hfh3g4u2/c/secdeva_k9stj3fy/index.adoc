== Foundation

The Foundation phase establishes the prerequisites for secure development. Complete these tasks before or in parallel with early development work — they ensure your team has the right people, knowledge, and context to make security decisions throughout the project.

.*What this phase covers*
[%collapsible]
====
[mermaid]
----
flowchart TB
    B[Analyse the business context]
    subgraph P[Roles]
      direction LR
      S[Assign security roles] --> I[Ensure competence]
    end
----

* *Analyse the business context* — Identify what you are protecting, who might attack it, and what damage could result. This drives all subsequent security decisions. Involve business stakeholders to security enhancement.
* *Assign security roles* — Make security responsibilities explicit by assigning accountable people to key roles.
* *Ensure competence* — Verify that team members have adequate cybersecurity knowledge for their roles, and arrange training where needed.
====
=== Why it matters

Without a clear business context, security efforts lack direction. Without assigned roles, tasks fall through the cracks. Without competence, even good processes produce weak results. Foundation addresses all three.
