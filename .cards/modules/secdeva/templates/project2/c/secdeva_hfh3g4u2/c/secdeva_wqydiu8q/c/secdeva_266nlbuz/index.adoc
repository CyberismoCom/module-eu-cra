This register captures identified security risks for your product.
Create a risk if you identify a _cyber threat_ — something that can cause harm by exploiting one or more vulnerabilities. Risks are assessed by their potential for loss: how likely a threat is to exploit a vulnerability, and how severe the resulting harm would be.

Each risk can lead to security requirements, design decisions, or testing activities. Without documented risks, security work lacks focus and prioritisation.

Risks are typically identified during threat modeling but can emerge at any point in the lifecycle.

[TIP] 
====
If the risk affects a certain element of the threat model or a certain asset, then you can create the risk within the affected card. 
====
